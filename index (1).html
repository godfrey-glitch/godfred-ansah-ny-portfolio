II

About

I am a Public Relations and Digital Communication specialist, currently serving as the Communication & Marketing Assistant at Africa Dyslexia Organization — where I manage social media, design newsletters, and run campaigns advocating for inclusive education across Africa.

I am also the Founder of Nyansa AI, a Ghana-localised AI educational platform for students aged 4–18, built on the GES curriculum and available in seven Ghanaian languages. I built it as a communications student because I saw a gap and refused to wait.

My background spans film production, youth leadership, ambassador and volunteer roles, and a deep personal connection to the causes I work for.
